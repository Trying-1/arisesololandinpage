No User Accounts or Cloud Profiles Maintained

Fast Image does not have an account creation, registration, or login system. We do not store user names, passwords, email addresses, or personal credentials on any remote servers.

1. Account Deletion Policy

Because Fast Image does not require user accounts, there are no remote accounts or credentials stored in our infrastructure to delete. All application usage is anonymous and local to your device.

2. How to Delete All Stored App Data

All project sequence metadata, imported assets, temporary frames, and configuration files are stored locally in your device's sandboxed storage. You have complete control to wipe this data at any moment:

1

Clearing In-App Data (Recommended)

Open the app, go to your project or sequence editor, tap Clear All on the images list, or delete saved projects from the project manager.

2

Via Android Device Settings

Go to your phone's Settings > Apps (or Application Manager) > Fast Image > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage (Clear Data). This immediately deletes all local projects, cached thumbnails, and preferences.

3

Uninstalling Fast Image

Uninstalling the Fast Image app from your device immediately prompts Android to completely erase all sandboxed app directories, databases, and cached assets.
